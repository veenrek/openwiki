## Abstract

Using the CLEO~II detector at CESR, we have measured the ratio of branching fractions ${\cal B}(D_s^+ \to φe^+ ν)/{\cal B}(D_s^+ \to φπ^+) = 0.54\pm 0.05\pm 0.04$. We use this measurement to obtain a model dependent estimate of ${\cal B}(D_s^+ \to φπ^+)$.
