## Abstract

Using the CLEO-II detector at CESR we have measured the ratio of branching fractions, ${\cal B}(D^+\rightarrow K^- π^+ π^+)/{\cal B}(D^0 \rightarrow K^-π^+) = 2.35 \pm 0.16 \pm 0.16$. Our recent measurement of ${\cal B}(D^0 \rightarrow K^-π^+)$ then gives ${\cal B}(D^+\rightarrow K^- π^+ π^+) = (9.3 \pm 0.6 \pm 0.8)\%$.
