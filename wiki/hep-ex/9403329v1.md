## Abstract

Branching ratios for the dominant Cabibbo-suppressed decays of the $τ$ lepton have been measured by CLEO~II in $e^+ e^-$ annihilation at CESR ($\sqrt{s} \sim 10.6$~GeV) using kaons with momenta below $0.7\ \rm GeV/c$. The inclusive branching ratio into one charged kaon is $(1.60 \pm 0.12 \pm 0.19)\%$. For the exclusive decays, $B(τ\to K^-) = (0.66 \pm 0.07 \pm 0.09)\%$, $B(τ\to K^- π^0) = (0.51 \pm 0.10 \pm 0.07)\%$, and, based on three events, $B(τ\to K^- π^0 π^0) < 0.3\%$ at the 90\% confidence level. These represent significant improvements over previous results. $B(τ\to K^- π^0)$ is measured for the first time with exclusive $π^0$ reconstruction.
