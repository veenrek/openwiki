## Abstract

Quantum interference effects between past and future events (neutral kaon transitions, including CP violating decays) are discussed for entangled kaon pairs produced through $φ\to K^0 \bar{K^0}$ in $φ$-factories. Contrasting with the exciting conclusions of other recent analyses, we predict the inexistence of such an observable effect.
