## Abstract

We suggest that Bose statistics for photons can be tested by looking for decays of spin-1 bosons into two photons. The experimental upper limit on the decay $Z \rightarrow γγ$ is used to establish for the first time the quantitative measure of the validity of Bose symmetry for photons.
