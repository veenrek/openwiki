## Abstract

In this paper, we analyze the solvability of the discrete nonlinear Schrödinger equation \begin{equation*} iβ(Δ_t+\nabla_t)φ(t,k) +γ|φ(t,k)|^2φ(t,k) +\varepsilon Δ_k^2φ(t,k-1) = g(t,φ(t,k)), \end{equation*} where $Δ_t$ and $Δ_k$ denote the standard forward difference operators in the variables $t$ and $k$, respectively, $\nabla_t$ denotes the standard backward difference operator in $t$, and \begin{equation*} Δ_k^2φ(t,k-1) = φ(t,k+1)-2φ(t,k)+φ(t,k-1) \end{equation*} is the discrete Laplacian operator in the spatial variable $k$. Throughout, we will assume the parameters $β$ and $\varepsilon$ are positive real numbers, the parameter $γ$ is a nonzero real number, and the potential function $g:\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{C}\to \mathbb{C}$ is continuous.
