## Abstract

We prove existence of solutions to a nonlinear degenerate elliptic equation of the form \[ \begin{cases} -Δ_{1} u+ \frac{|D u|}{(1-u)^γ}=g & \mbox{in $Ω$,}\\ u=0 \hfill & \mbox{on $\partialΩ$,} \end{cases} \] in a suitable sense, where $Ω$ is a bounded open set of $\mathbb{R}^n$, $γ>0$ is a fixed parameter, $g\geq 0 $ is a function in some Lebesgue space.
