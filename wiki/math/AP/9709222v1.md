## Abstract

We prove local well-posedness results for the semi-linear wave equation for data in $H^γ$, $0 < γ< \frac{n-3}{2(n-1)}$, extending the previously known results for this problem. The improvement comes from an introduction of a two-scale Lebesgue space $X^{r,p}_k$.
