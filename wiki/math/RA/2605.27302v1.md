## Abstract

Our aim is to study matrix polynomials over max-algebras and their growth in terms of a max-induced semi-norm. We investigate the relationship between the asymptotic growth of polynomial products and the joint spectral radius of the associated coefficient set. Dynamics of max-linear maps and convergence to periodic points under a single joint spectral radius condition and the existence of common max-eigenvectors of the coefficient pool is also brought out.
