## Abstract

The Kodaira energy of a polarized manifold (M,L) is defined by κε(M,L)=-Inf{t\in Q|κ(K+tL)\ge 0}. Here we propose a couple of conjectures and announce several partial results. 3-dimensional cases are mainly considered. A hard copy is available on request to the author.
