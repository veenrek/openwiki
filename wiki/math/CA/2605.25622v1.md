## Abstract

Adopting the powerful methods introduced in \cite{li2021carnotcaratheodory, LZ2025}, we investigate the asymptotic behaviour at infinity for the heat kernel associated with the Grushin operator $Δ_G = Δ_x + |x|^2 Δ_u$ on $ \mathbb{R}_x ^n \times \mathbb{R}_u ^{n'} $ for all $ n , \, n' \ge 1 $. We further establish sharp bounds for its spatial derivatives. As a by-product, precise estimates and small-time asymptotic behaviour for the Grushin heat kernel will be provided, as explicit as one can possibly hope for.
