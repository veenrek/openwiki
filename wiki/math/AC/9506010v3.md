## Abstract

In this paper, I compute the last non-trivial term of the minimal free resolution of the homogeneous ideal of $s$ points of $P^n$ in sufficiently general position, for any $s$, showing that this term is the one conjectured by the Minimal Resolution Conjecture of Anna Lorenzini. I use a geometrical method, the "vectorial méthode d'Horace" developped by André Hirschowitz and Carlos Simpson to get a proof of the MRC for $s$ large enough.
