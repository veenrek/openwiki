## Abstract

We study a symmetry problem for the $h$-polynomials of edge rings of bipartite graphs. Let $G$ be a bipartite graph and write $h(\mathbb{k}[G];t)=h_0+h_1t+\cdots+h_st^s$. We prove that if $\Bbbk[G]$ is pseudo-Gorenstein and $h_1=h_{s-1}$, then $\Bbbk[G]$ is Gorenstein. Equivalently, under these assumptions the $h$-polynomial of $\Bbbk[G]$ is palindromic. The proof treats the $2$-connected case first by translating the numerical condition $h_1=h_{s-1}$ into a tight-separation condition for non-edges, and then passes to arbitrary bipartite graphs using the block decomposition. We also construct a blockwise Gorenstein closure, obtained by adjoining all non-edges not separated by tight acceptable sets, and show that this construction preserves the next-to-leading coefficient of the $h$-polynomial.
