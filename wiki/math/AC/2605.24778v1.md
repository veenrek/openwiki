## Abstract

In this note, we give a characterization of all projectable and divisible-projectable reduced $f$-rings satisfying the first convexity property and admitting elimination of quantifiers, in the language of lattice-ordered rings with the divisibility relation, the radical relation associated to the minimal prime spectrum, and the local divisibility relation.
