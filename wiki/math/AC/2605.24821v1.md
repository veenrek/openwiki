## Abstract

Let $\Fq$ be the finite field with $q$ elements. We study the number of $\Fq$-rational points on Danielewski and double Danielewski surfaces. For Danielewski surfaces, the point count is reduced to the number of roots of $P(Z)$ over $\Fq.$ For double Danielewski surfaces, one has to count the number of tuples $(\be,\g)\in\Fq^2$, such that $P(0,\g)=0$, $Q(0,\be,\g)=0$ hold simultaneously. We compute these numbers using gcd methods, resultants, character sums, Gauss sums, and the König--Rados theorem. We obtain explicit formulas in several structured cases, derive general bounds, and give a Macaulay2 algorithm for verification and show an intresting connection between the number of $\Fq$-rational points of these surfaces and polygonal numbers.
