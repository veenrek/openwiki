## Abstract

We use the upper and lower potential functions and Bowen's formula estimating the Hausdorff dimension of the limit set of a regular semigroup generated by finitely many $C^{1+α}$-contracting mappings. This result is an application of the geometric distortion lemma in the first paper at this series.
