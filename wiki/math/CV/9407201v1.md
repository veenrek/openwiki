## Abstract

In the paper we give some necessary conditions for a mapping to be a $κ$-geodesic in non-convex complex ellipsoids. Using these results we calculate explicitly the Kobayashi metric in the ellipsoids $\{|z_1|^2+|z_2|^{2m}<1\}\subset\bold C^2$, where $m<\frac12$.
