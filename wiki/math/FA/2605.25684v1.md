## Abstract

For operators defined on locally convex spaces we define the notions of boundedness and ergodicity associated to an infinite matrix. Given two matrices $ A$ and $ B$, we study when $ A$-bounded operators are $ B$-ergodic. Using this approach we obtain equivalent formulations for the classical notions of power boundedness, Cesàro boundedness and mean ergodicity.
