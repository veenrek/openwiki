## Abstract

We introduce a concentration property for probability measures on $\scriptstyle{R^n}$, which we call Property~($\scriptstyleτ$); we show that this property has an interesting stability under products and contractions (Lemmas 1,~2,~3). Using property~($\scriptstyleτ$), we give a short proof for a recent deviation inequality due to Talagrand. In a third section, we also recover known concentration results for Gaussian measures using our approach.}
