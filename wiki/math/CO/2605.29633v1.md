## Abstract

We derive the local and central limit theorems for the Stirling numbers of the second kind by elementary means, obtaining as corollaries effective asymptotic estimates for the Bell numbers and for the moments of the distribution. We also develop asymptotic expansions along several directions, all based on a novel finite-differencing approach; this provides the first self-contained elementary justification of such expansions.
