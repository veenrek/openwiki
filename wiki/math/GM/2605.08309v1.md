## Abstract

In this note, we derive an elementary version of the coarea formula by considering the mass of a solid body with density $g (x)$. Then we present an rigorous proof using the changing variable formula. To this end we construct the diffeomorphism $Φ$ via the gradient flow and compute its Jacobian determinant via geometric method.
