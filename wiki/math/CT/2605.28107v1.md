## Abstract

K. S. S. Nambooripad introduced an interesting class of categories known as normal categories, which are categories with subobjects, morphisms admitting factorization and having sufficiently many cones. These normal categories plays fundamental role in the study of structure of regular semigroups. In [6] we discussed the category of chain bundles and category of chains. In the present paper revisits classical notions of bundles, including fibre bundles, vector bundles, and principal G-bundles, and discuss the chain categories arising from the category of bundles. Moreover, its is verified that these categories are categories with subobjects.
