## Abstract

In virtue of the Chubykalo - Smirnov-Rueda generalized form of the Maxwell-Lorentz equation a new form of the energy density of the electromagnetic field was obtained. This result allows us to explain a physical origin of the Oppenheimer-Ahluwalia zero-energy solutions of the Maxwell equations.
