## Abstract

This paper describes a new framework in educational technology - the World Wide Student Laboratory (WWSL) Project. WWSL is a dynamic international Internet-based collaboration of resourses of educational and research labs of universities, institutions, government research centers and companies. The concepts of the WWSL are outlined, a possible idea for structure is discussed and examples of various WWSL studies are shown.
